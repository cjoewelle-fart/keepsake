# Naming round 6: plain words around Unjumble, Homecoming and the homing pigeon (Oct 27, 2026)

The owner likes Unjumble, finds Glassine too unfamiliar and Homecoming a little vague, and likes the homing-pigeon idea. They asked for common, non-technical options. These are web searches, not legal clearance.

## Three findings

1. **Close competitor: PHOTO home.** A free, offline Windows app (GPL-3.0, v1.0.0, Oct 14, 2026). It sorts photos into Year/Month folders, copies by default, never deletes, has one-click undo and puts undated photos in `_UNDATED`. It isn't built for PhotoRec output or junk cleanup. The Team Lead checked the listing. It's in `team/research/competitors.md`, and decision D22 asks the owner whether it changes gate 1. "Photos Home" is out as a name for the same reason.
2. **Homing can't be used.** The HOMING registration is live (Reg. 6246113, Homing, Inc., class 42 software, homing-pigeon logo). Any "Homing ___" name sits even closer to it. A pigeon *mascot* under another name is fine, as long as it has no envelopes or letters.
3. **Unjumble has a bigger risk than round 4 found.**
   - A free Android word game called Unjumble has been on F-Droid since Oct 2024. Tribune's JUMBLE is a well-known puzzle brand.
   - Both are word games, not photo tools, so this doesn't rule Unjumble out. The test should ask "Is this a word game?", and the attorney must check both names.
   - The writer now notes that "jumble" may be less everyday for Filipino speakers than round 5 assumed, so the name test should check this.

## Descriptor

A descriptor such as "Unjumble: photo sorter for recovered files" helps search and app stores, and fixes vagueness. It doesn't help or hurt the trademark. Use it in titles and listings, not inside app sentences.

## The team's recommendation

- **Test set:** Unjumble, Home Album, Homebird and Homecoming (photo sorter), with Keepsake as the control. Show Homebird and Homecoming with the descriptor so the comparison is fair.
- **First question for each name:** "What do you think this app does?", then "Would it send your photos anywhere?" A name fails if 2 or more of 5 testers say it recovers, deletes, sends or shares photos, or is a word game.
- **Unshuffle** stays Unjumble's backup.
- **Before the beta:** the attorney search covers the winning name, plus JUMBLE/Unjumble, HOMEBIRD and HOMECOMING as relevant.
