# Naming round 20: the owner's top seven (Nov 10, 2026)

The owner's top choices: Sortory, Recollect, Photory, Keepsake, Yearloom, Refound, Restorely. Sortory, Photory and Yearloom come from round 19. The UX writer reviewed the three new names for sound, meaning and honesty (no web searches). The Product owner web-screened Recollect, Refound, Restorely, and Keepsake, which had never been screened in any earlier round. This is not legal clearance. Domains, IPOPHL and WIPO couldn't be checked from here.

## Findings

1. **Keepsake is probably not registrable.** It's the working name and the test control, but it was never screened before. Two live class 9 marks (KEEPSAKE, KEEPSAFE), a same-sounding family-photo app with a large user base, and a refused KEEPSAKE application for a photo app. Whether we could keep using it unregistered is the attorney's call. This makes settling a new name more important, not less.
2. **Names that sound like recovery work against us.** Refound and Restorely promise what the FAQ says we don't do ("Does it recover my photos? No."). Our users have just lost photos, so they are the most likely to be let down. The owner's "found again" idea can still live in copy: "Your photos were in there all along. Now they're in order."
3. **Recollect is honest but taken in our class and field.**
4. **Sortory and Yearloom are the only two of the seven that are clean on both counts.**
