# Naming round 5: movement names (Oct 27, 2026)

The owner likes Unjumble and asked for names about movement and ease, inspired by Slidebox. These are web searches, not legal clearance.

## Why Unjumble (rationale)

- **It names what people see.** A PhotoRec folder is thousands of f1234567.jpg files in dozens of recup_dir folders. "Jumbled" is how that looks, and "un-" says the app puts it right.
- **It says what the app does without overpromising.** It doesn't promise recovery or repair (no "fix", "rescue" or "restore"), and it doesn't suggest deleting or cleaning. Untangle (the owner's idea) has the same meaning, but its name is taken.
- **It's familiar to Filipino users.** "Unjumble the words" is a standard Philippine school exercise, so many users will know the exact word.
- **It reads naturally in app copy.** "Unjumble can't save to that folder. Nothing was lost…" and "Try Unjumble. It turned that pile of f1234567.jpg files into folders by date." It's playful but not silly, so it fits "warm, not cute".
- **It's easy to say and type when heard.** The main risk is "Unjumbled".
- **Its risks:** word-game apps use similar names, and there's a small old utility called Unjumble (round 4). The attorney search must clear it before the beta.

## Slidebox and the swipe category

Slidebox is a "Tinder for your camera roll" photo cleaner: swipe up to delete, tap an album to file. The swipe category (Swipewipe, Swiper, CleanSwipe, Swipe & Delete…) is all about deleting. Swipe-, slide-, wipe-, clean- or -box names would tell people "this app deletes photos", against **Never destructive**.

**A product fact the name must respect:** Keepsake is a desktop web app where you tick and untick photos on a timeline, with no swiping. A movement name only fits if the movement is what the app does: files falling into year and month folders. Moving the review screen to swiping would be a scope decision for the owner, after launch at the earliest. Noted here, not recommended. voice.md also bans "effortless" in copy.

## Shortlist

| Name | Idea | Conflicts | Writer's read |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Unshuffle** | Recovery shuffled the deck; the app deals it back in date order | A small Playdate game; "pixel_unshuffle" (a machine-learning term) | Natural and calm, but people think of music shuffle first, and it can read like an undo button. Keep as Unjumble's backup. |
| **Into Place** | "It all falls into place" | None found; a common phrase, hard to protect | Warmest, but breaks in sentences ("Into Place can't save…"). Better as a tagline. |
| Unscatter | Scattered files gathered into one library | None found | Not a word people use; does Unjumble's job less well. Drop. |
| Homeslide | Photos slide home | None found | Suggests swiping, and sounds like "homeslice". Drop. |
| Dropfold | Each photo drops into its folder | None found | "Drop" sounds like losing or deleting, and echoes Dropbox (cloud). Drop. |

**Dropped (20):** Sortbox, Slotbox, Driftbox, Glidebox, Settled/Settle (SETTLED. marks for home-organization software), Slotted (a 2024 class 9 filing), Fileaway, Datedrop, Lineup, Trickle, Restack, Unpile, Timeslide/Dayslide, Riffle, Shuffle, Dealt, Tumble, Nestle, Flock/Roundup, Inplace. The Product owner's notes have the links.

## The team's view

- **None of the movement names beats Unjumble.** The sense of movement is best carried by Unjumble plus a tagline: "Unjumble: your photos, into place by date." In testing, check that "in place" isn't read as "my photos are recovered".
- **Unshuffle is Unjumble's backup** if Unjumble fails the attorney search. Don't test two "Un-" names side by side, because it splits the signal.
- **Test set unchanged:** Unjumble, Homecoming and Glassine, with Keepsake as the control (D21).
- **Test design:**
  - Ask "What do you think this app does?" A name fails if 2 or more of 5 testers say it deletes, cleans or recovers photos, or that you swipe.
  - Ask testers to type the name they just heard.
