# Naming round 3: conceptual names (Oct 27, 2026)

The owner asked for names that are more conceptual than Keepsake, Keepies and Tuckaway. Seeds: "Wizard" (the app sorts the files as if by magic) and "Nimbus" (a sky cloud, and a play on "the cloud"). The Product owner screened 27 names with web searches, and the UX writer read the shortlist in real app copy. These are web searches, not legal clearance. The attorney search (US and Philippines) still comes before the beta.

## The owner's seeds

- **Nimbus: high risk, not recommended.** The name means cloud, but our main promise is that nothing goes to the cloud, so every privacy explanation would start by contradicting it. The software field is also crowded: Nimbus Data (storage, live marks), Nimbus Capture/Note, Adobe's "Project Nimbus" (cloud Lightroom).
- **Wizard: high risk, not recommended.** People who lose photos almost always come across EaseUS *Data Recovery Wizard*, a well-known paid tool, and several "Photo Recovery Wizard" look-alikes. "Magic" means hidden reasons, which clashes with "explain every decision in plain words". "Setup wizard" sounds technical.
- **Ideas kept from both:** the wizard's helper (*Familiar*), skill rather than mystery; sky names that don't mean cloud storage (*Sundog*, *Lodestar*).

## Shortlist

| Name | Idea | Conflicts found | Risk | Say it and type it | Writer's read |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Glassine** | The see-through paper sleeve that protects negatives and prints. Fits the Quiet Prints envelope look. You can see every decision, and nothing is harmed. | No app or software mark found. Glass (a photo community app) is a near neighbour. | Low | Medium: unfamiliar word, "glass-EEN" or "GLASS-ine" | Best fit with our voice and the honesty promise; needs a tagline ("a clear sleeve for your photos") |
| **Familiar** | The wizard's helper: skill, not mystery. Also "familiar faces". Echoes Filipino *pamilyar* and *pamilya* (family). | Familiar was a family photo-sharing app that shut down in 2013. A common word, so hard to search for and to protect. | Medium | Low–medium: often misspelled "familar" | Warmest name, but it reads like an adjective in sentences ("Familiar can't save…", "Try Familiar") |
| **Lodestar** | A guiding star: calm guidance through the mess. | Marks found are dead. There's a Lodestar astro camera and Lodestar Systems, plus a LOADSTAR mark. | Medium | Medium: may be typed "Loadstar" | Clearest to read and say; grand rather than warm |
| **Sundog** | A bright spot of light beside the sun: the sky idea without cloud storage. | Sundog Software (sky rendering for flight simulators). No photo app found. | Medium | Low | Too cute: "dog" in an error message feels flippant to someone upset |
| **Varve** | Yearly layers of lake mud that geologists count to date things: "sorted into years". | None found | Low | High: heard as "verve" or "valve" | Too obscure; drop |

**Dropped (22):** Strata, Cairn, Riffle, Almanac, Daybook, Lantern, Argent, Cosmos, Tessera, Quire, Steward, Heirloom, Trove, Kith, Knack, Merlin, Brownie (Kodak), Sunprint, Winnow (live marks, and it means throwing things away), Sextant, Hob, Aurora, Halo, Zenith, Prism. All of them already exist as apps or brands, or have a bad meaning. The Product owner's full notes have the links.

**For competitor tracking:** Riffle is a local, no-cloud photo culling tool ("Thousands of photos. Keep what counts"). It doesn't sort PhotoRec output.

## The team's view

- **Product owner:** test Glassine, Sundog and Familiar.
- **UX writer:** test Glassine, Familiar and Lodestar. Sundog leans cute.
- **Team Lead:** goes with the writer. Sundog's main problem is the voice ("warm, not cute"), and that is the writer's call.
- **Both:** keep Keepsake as the control. In the writer's words, none of the five beats Keepsake on being understood the moment someone sees it. If no new name clearly beats it in the test, that is useful to know.
- **Test the sentences, not just the bare name.** Include the error line ("___ can't save to that folder") and a friend's recommendation, because those are where Familiar and Sundog break down.

The name is the owner's call: decision D21 on the dashboard.
