# Brand voice (Oct 15, 2026)

By the UX writer. Name-agnostic until the rename (D5) lands: `[Name]` = product name, `{n}` = a real count.

## Voice

| Attribute | Sounds like | Never sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| Calm | "Take your time. Nothing here will be deleted." | "Act fast! Recover your memories before it's too late!" |
| Honest | "{n} files look damaged. Some may still show part of a picture." | "All your photos are back!" |
| Careful | "We set these aside. You can bring any of them back." | "Junk removed. Library optimized." |
| Warm, not cute | "That folder didn't open. Your files are fine." | "Oops! Something went wrong 😅" |

## Words

Use: photos, files, copies, folder, your computer, set aside, keep, save, safe, nothing deleted, nothing uploaded, check, by date, "you can change this".

| Avoid | Say instead |
|---|---|
| seamless, effortless, smart, powerful, magic, revolutionary | what it does: "sorts your photos by date" |
| unlock, unleash, supercharge, elevate | see, save, find |
| securely, military-grade, 100% safe | the fact: "never leaves your computer" |
| delete, remove, clean (for set-aside files) | set aside |
| duplicates, thumbnails, metadata, EXIF | copies, small preview copies, date taken |
| restore, rescue (as a promise) | find, sort, save |
| "let's dive in", "we've got you covered", "worry-free", "peace of mind" | cut it |

## Promise and taglines

- Brand promise (recommended): "Your photos stay on your computer, nothing is deleted, and you decide what to keep."
- Tagline (recommended): "Sorted by date. Never uploaded."
- Alternatives: "Your photos, found and kept safe." · "Every photo stays yours."

## Key moments

- Start: "Let's find the photos in your recovered files." / "Choose the folder your recovery program made. [Name] reads it on this computer and never changes it."
- Scanning: "Checked {n} of {total} files. This is all happening on your computer. You can stop any time, and nothing will be changed."
- Results: "We found {n} photos and videos worth keeping." / "They're sorted by the date each one was taken."
- Nothing deleted: "We set aside {n} files that look like leftovers from the recovery. Nothing was deleted. Your recovery folder is exactly as it was, and you can keep any of these."
- Export done: "Your {n} photos and videos are saved." / "They're in [folder name], sorted into a folder for each year and month. Your recovery folder hasn't been touched." Button: "Open my photos". (Check against the real export layout.)
- Error: "We couldn't open that folder." / "Your files are fine. Nothing was changed. If the folder is on a memory card or USB drive, check that it's still plugged in, then try again." Button: "Choose the folder again".
- Privacy, one line: "Your photos never leave this computer, and [Name] never changes or deletes your files."
- Privacy, three points: Nothing is uploaded. Nothing is deleted. Nothing is hidden (free and open source).

## Show trust, don't claim it

1. State facts, not feelings. If a sentence could be on any app's site, rewrite it.
2. Show the numbers, and make them add up on screen.
3. Say what we don't do: no account, no upload, no deleting.
4. Tell people how to check (Wi-Fi off still works; link to the code). Publish the Wi-Fi line only after QA confirms offline works end to end.
5. Admit limits early. Never say "all your photos".
