# Naming round 16: Keepsake re-check and "binder" names (Nov 2, 2026)

The owner asked two things on Nov 2: (1) is Keepsake safe to use (T44, R6/D5), and (2) could the name use "binder", from the Pokémon card-collecting craze where people keep their cards in binders. The Product owner checked each name with web searches: app stores, software brands, US marks in classes 9/42, and photo and collection apps. These are web searches, not legal clearance. IPOPHL's database could not be reached through web search. The attorney search (USPTO and IPOPHL, classes 9 and 42) still comes before the beta (phase 4).

## Findings first

1. **Keepsake is crowded in photo apps, and the USPTO has already refused one KEEPSAKE app filing.** Keepsake Frames (Keepsake Inc., Photo & Video, "70,000+ five-star reviews") filed KEEPSAKE in class 9 for its app (87246571). That filing was abandoned after ex parte appeal in 2018. The reason isn't visible on the web.
2. **An open-source "Keepsake" already does part of our job.** It is a desktop app on GitHub that downloads children's photos, restores their dates (EXIF) and skips duplicates.
3. **KEEPSAFE is a live class 9 registration (Reg. 4820511) for software that hides photos.** It is one letter away from our name and also has a privacy pitch.
4. **"Binder" now reads as a trading-card app.** The app stores have Binder: Sports Card Tracker, Kollect: Trading Card Binder, TCG Binder, MTG Binder and Card Binder: MTG Manager. BINDERPOS, a registered 9/42 mark, sells store software to card shops.
5. **Binder sounds like Bynder.** Bynder is a large image-asset software company with a US filing in classes 9, 35, 38, 41 and 42.

## 1. Keepsake: verdict

| Use found | Field | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Keepsake Frames (Keepsake Inc.) | Photo & Video app: framed prints | Class 9 filing 87246571 abandoned after appeal. Keepsake Inc. also holds a 1983 KEEPSAKE ring mark (73446076) |
| Keepsakes App (Fractal Works) | Photo & Video: printed photo books | Live on iOS |
| Keepsake (Chicago startup) | iPhone app that turns photos into albums | Prototype stage |
| Keepsake (GitHub, ss44) | Open-source desktop photo downloader that fixes dates | Finding 2 |
| Keepsake Collections | Lifestyle app with photos of belongings | First released May 2026 |
| Keepsake (voice reminders) | iPhone memory app | Not in our field |
| Qeepsake (Tinybeans) | Family photo journal, about 15K ratings | Sounds the same |
| KEEPSAFE Reg. 4820511 | Class 9 photo-hiding software | Finding 3 |
| KEEPSAKE 90904771 | Class 39: custody of digital business data | Filed 2021, not yet examined |

**Verdict: high risk as the final name for a photo app.** I found no live KEEPSAKE registration for photo software in classes 9/42. The legal risk is still at least medium: one KEEPSAKE app filing was refused, and KEEPSAFE is registered. The bigger risk is that people won't find us: the photo-app results are full of other Keepsakes. Keep it as the T44 control (D21) and don't launch under it unless the attorney says it is clear.

## 2. Binder names

| Name | Idea | In a sentence | Say / spell risk | Conflicts found | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Binder** | Every photo in its pocket, in order | "Binder found 2,214 photos." | Medium: "Bynder"; Filipino reading-pronunciation "bin-der" | Binder: Sports Card Tracker; Bynder (image-asset software); BINDERPOS (registered 9/42); LiveBinders (registered 9/42); mybinder.org; Android Binder; Binder by Magaya. Also an insurance binder and a chest binder | High |
| **Photo Binder / PhotoBinder** | A binder of photos | "Photo Binder found 2,214 photos." | Low | Photo Binder (iOS) merges photos into one PDF, which also hints at changing files (**Never destructive**). PixBinder Mac image organizer. Too descriptive to register | High |
| **Memory Binder** | Your memories, filed | "Memory Binder found 2,214 photos." | Low–medium: "memory" can mean computer memory | **Acer MemoryBinder**, a photo-organizing app from a big PC brand in the Philippines. "Memory binders" is a scrapbooking term | High |
| **Snapbinder** | Snaps into a binder | "Snapbinder found 2,214 photos." | Low | Jalema Snapbinder filing product; trucking snap binders; Snap Inc.'s oppositions (round 12) | High |
| **Keepsake Binder** | Carries on from Keepsake | "Keepsake Binder found 2,214 photos." | Low, but 4 syllables | Every Keepsake conflict above; a common craft-printable term | High |
| **Yearbinder** | A binder of your years, one page per month | "Yearbinder found 2,214 photos." "Yearbinder can't save to that folder. Your files are fine." | Medium: "Year Binder", "Yearbynder"; may be read "Yearb-inder" | Only Thomson Reuters ONESOURCE tax-year "binders" and a "Year of Dates Binder" printable. No app or mark found | Low–medium |

**Fit:**
- **Good:** a card binder is a grid of fixed pockets with nothing taken out. That matches round 15's month-grid logo idea, and nothing in the word suggests uploading or deleting (**Private by architecture**, **Never destructive**).
- **Bad:**
  - In app stores in 2026, "binder" means trading cards.
  - "Bind" can suggest merging files (Photo Binder makes one PDF).
  - The craze is a trend, while our users are stressed people of all ages and repair shops.
  - Many native English speakers know "binder" as an office or school word.
- **Speaking and typing:** most Filipino and native English speakers know "binder" as a school supply, so typing it is easy. When heard, expect "Bynder" and, among Filipino testers, "bin-der".

## Proposed change to the test set (D21, T44 rules)

| Slot | Name | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Control | **Keepsake** | No change |
| Owner's favourite | **Unjumble** | No change |
| Literal, result | **Reshelf** | No change |
| Search word | **Sortwell** | No change |
| Years in order | **Yeartree** | No change |
| Owner's binder idea | **Yearbinder** | New, 6th name (the maximum for 1½ hours) |

- **Sessions:** never test Yearbinder and Yeartree in the same session (both are "year" names). The round 15 rules still apply.
- **"What do you think this app does?"** Also ask "Is this for trading cards?" and "Would it combine your photos into one file?" It fails if 2 or more of 5 testers say yes to either.
- **Hear it, type it:** count "Year Binder", "Yearbynder" and "Yearbinda" as fails.
- **If the owner wants 5 names:** Yearbinder replaces Yeartree, which already carries the "Year Three" risk.

## Next in line

1. Yearline (round 13) if both year names fail.
2. Binder, Photo Binder, Memory Binder, Snapbinder and Keepsake Binder are dropped.
3. Keep "binder" as a picture (a month grid of pockets) for visual direction (phase 3), whatever the name.

The name is the owner's call (D21), and it must be settled before the beta (phase 4).
