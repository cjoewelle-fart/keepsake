# Naming round 19: the owner's eight (Nov 10, 2026)

The owner felt the current test list was weak and suggested eight names: Sortory, Trove, Collectory, Nestory, Memolio, Photory, Yearloom and Talebox. The UX writer reviewed how each one sounds, reads and what it means (no web searches). The Product owner ran a fresh web screen (apps, brands, public trademark pages, search crowding). This is not legal clearance: the attorney's search (US and Philippines, classes 9 and 42) and domain checks still have to run.

**Couldn't verify:** domains (only trove.com was confirmed active), IPOPHL (not searchable from here), WIPO pages (none surfaced).

## Findings

1. **Three of the four "-story" blends lose "story" when spoken.** Sortory, Photory and Collectory sound like *history* or *factory*. People mostly hear the name from a friend or a repair shop, so the feeling half is often lost (round 18, finding 4).
2. **"Story" means posting online for many users** (Facebook and Instagram Stories). Daystory was dropped in round 18 for this.
3. **The crowded names are crowded in our exact field.** Trove, Nestory/Nestori, Memolio and Photory all have photo or organizing products already.
4. **Sortory is the one name that adds something the current list lacks:** a made-up, ownable "sort" name without Sortly's "-ly" problem. It's the natural swap for Sortlay or Sortlee, which test the same idea.
5. **The writer's view:** the current nine are plain rather than weak, and plain is what passes hear-it, type-it with Filipino testers. If the list lacks feeling, a tagline ("Sorted by date. Never uploaded.") may add it more safely than a riskier name.

## If Sortory or Yearloom join the name test (T44)

- **Sortory:** the owner picks one "right" reading before the test (writer suggests "SORT-or-ee"). Fails: Sortery, Sortary, Sortori, Shortory, Sort Story. Extra question: "Does this app post your photos online, or make stories or videos from them?" Never in the same block as Sortlee/Sortlay, SortnOrg or another "-ory" name.
- **Yearloom:** fails: Heirloom, Earloom, Yearlum. Extra question: "Does this app join your photos together or make a video?" Never in the same block as Keepsake or another Year- name.
- A name fails "what does it do?" if 2 or more of 5 say yes to the extra question.
