# Naming round 4: the owner's new directions (Oct 27, 2026)

The owner suggested Untangle, Deets, File Banana and File Pigeon, plus everyday phrases (like "bring home the bacon") as a source of ideas. The Product owner reviewed each one, used it as a starting point for new names, and screened them all with web searches. The UX writer then read the shortlist in real app sentences. These are web searches, not legal clearance; the attorney search (US and Philippines, classes 9 and 42) still comes before the beta.

## The owner's ideas

| Idea | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Untangle | Good idea; the name is taken | Untangle Inc. network-security software (now Arista) holds US marks, and there are UNTANGLED marks. Untangling means sorting, not repairing, so the idea is honest; "fix" must never appear in our copy. |
| Deets | No | Existing Deets apps (contacts and file sharing, 2012; restaurant reviews, 2023) and Deetz (events). "Details" means file data, not photos. Many people learning English won't know the word, and heard aloud it's typed "DEET" (bug spray). Writer: too cute for someone upset. |
| File Banana | No | "Going bananas" means panic. "Banana" next to images now suggests Google's Nano Banana AI editor. Writer: "File Banana can't save to that folder" makes a joke of someone's worst day. |
| File Pigeon | No, but the homing idea is kept | "Pigeon – file transfer" app and Digital Pigeon. A mail pigeon sends files away, which contradicts "nothing leaves your computer". The homing pigeon, which always flies home, led to Homecoming. |
| Phrases | "Bring home the bacon" means earning money | The "coming home" family of phrases fits better: Homecoming. |

## New names by direction (✓ = kept)

- **From Untangle:** ✓ Unjumble, ✓ Detangle. Dropped Unscramble (word-game apps), Unmuddle (Unfuddle), Unknot (heard as "un-not").
- **Slang:** ✓ Phew (weak: heard as "few"). Dropped Keepers (an app called "Keepers – Keep only your best photos"), Throwback, Sorted, Faves, Stash.
- **Fruit:** ✓ Windfall, with Jamjar as a reserve. Dropped Pomegranate, Pomelo (Pomelo Filters photo app), Plum, Orchard, Clementine.
- **Pigeon:** ✓ Homeward, ✓ Homing (a live class 42 mark with a pigeon logo). Dropped Roost, Dovecote, Pigeonhole, Homer, Homebound.
- **Phrases:** ✓ Homecoming. Dropped Finders Keepers (a 2024 filing; also suggests recovery), Lost and Found, Shipshape, Ducks in a Row, Safe and Sound, Squared Away.

## Shortlist

| Name | Idea | Conflicts | Risk | Writer's read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Homecoming** | Scattered files come home to a dated library on your own computer, with nothing uploaded | No software product found. Search is crowded (school homecomings, the Spider-Man film, Beyoncé's "Homecoming") and the word is hard to protect | Low–medium | The warmest name in any round, without being cute. Filipinos know the word from alumni homecomings and OFWs coming home. It may suggest the photos are "back", so it needs a tagline about sorting. |
| **Unjumble** | Names the f1234567.jpg mess and its fix | Word-game apps; a small old utility | Medium | Clearest about what the app does. Only Keepsake matches it on instant understanding. Playful, not silly. |
| Detangle | Careful untangling; nothing is cut | DETANGLE IT mark (probably hair care) | Medium | Drop: means hair care first |
| Homeward | Photos on their way home | Homeward Inc. (real estate); roofing marks | Medium | Drop: a direction word, odd in sentences ("Sorted by Homeward") |
| Windfall | Scattered fruit gathered up | Windfall Data; a class 9 mark that may have lapsed | Medium | Drop: people hear money or luck; flippant |

## The team's view

- **Both the Product owner and the UX writer** recommend testing **Glassine, Homecoming and Unjumble**, with Keepsake as the control.
- **Why these three:** each is a different kind of name: a craft metaphor (Glassine), an emotional one (Homecoming) and a literal one (Unjumble).
- **The round 3 names:** Homecoming replaces Familiar, doing the same job without the "Familiar can't save…" problem. Lodestar drops out as "grand, not warm". Familiar stays as the backup.
- **Test design:** test whole sentences, including the error line and a friend's recommendation. For Homecoming, also ask testers what they expect the app to do: if they think it gets lost photos back, the name overpromises.

The name is the owner's call: decision D21.
