# Naming, round 2 (Oct 16, 2026)

By the Product owner, answering the owner's D12 note ("Why Keepfold again? What's the rationale? Shall we think of more options?").

## Why Keepfold, honestly
Keep + fold (as in folder): says what the app does without promising recovery, and it was the only finalist with no product or mark found. But we recommended it twice mostly because its search was clean, not because it sounds best. The "pf" in the middle is hard to say ("Keyfold", "Kip-pold"), it isn't warm, and "fold" can suggest laundry.

## Criteria
1–2 syllables preferred; type-it-after-hearing-it-once; no "recover/rescue" promise; not generic tech; fits the calm Quiet Prints look.

## Checked and dropped (web search, not legal clearance)
Sift (PhotoSift), Glean (software company), Tidy (iOS photo cleaner), Hush (photo vault apps), Fixer (Photo Fixer; implies repair), Proofsheet (Mac app), Ayos (AYOS PH app in our market), Sinop (Turkish city dominates search), Itabi/Ligpit (hard for English speakers).
