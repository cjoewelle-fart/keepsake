## Not generic AI

Avoid: purple/blue gradients and glow blobs, glassmorphism, sparkle or "AI" icons, Inter/Geist everywhere, cream + serif + terracotta, everything centred, emoji headers, three identical feature cards, stock floating people, confetti.
Do instead: flat fills with real thumbnails as the colour, 1px borders, say exactly what happened with numbers, left-aligned reading column, one main action, photo-world objects (prints, envelopes, contact sheets), a quiet receipt on completion.

## The three directions

| | A · Ledger | B · Photo Envelope (team pick) | C · Still Water |
|---|---|---|---|
| Idea | Every step ends in a receipt that adds up | Photos come back like prints from the photo shop | One quiet thing per screen |
| Mood | Exact, sober, accountable | Familiar, kind, tangible | Hushed, spacious, steady |
| Ground / surface | #F2F3F1 / #FFFFFF | #F4F1EB / #FFFFFF | #ECF1F0 / #FAFCFB |
| Ink / muted | #111614 / #4A524F | #1E1A2B / #575166 | #1B2826 / #4B5C59 |
| Primary | #0B5A3E vault green | #2A3F9E envelope blue | #23596B lagoon |
| Second colour | #8C4A00 amber text on #FFF4E0 | #F2B035 marigold (fill); #7A4A00 on #FFF3D6 | #3D6B52 sage on #E3EFE8 |
| Type | Archivo SemiCondensed + Public Sans + IBM Plex Mono | Bricolage Grotesque + Atkinson Hyperlegible (+ Doto for date stamps) | Red Hat Display + Red Hat Text |
| Shape | 8px, hairlines, no shadow | 14px cards, prints with 4px white border and soft shadow | 20px, tone only, no borders |
| Signature | "Deleted: 0" always in the header; printable tally | Orange film-camera date stamp on prints | Photos sharpen from blur as they're found |
| Risk | Cold for a grieving parent | Kitsch if illustrations multiply | Too passive; looks like a wellness app |

All text pairs meet WCAG AA (4.5:1), per the designer's calculation.

**Team recommendation:** B, plus A's tally slip as one component. B's identity comes from photos themselves, so it can't be mistaken for a fintech or wellness template; it evolves the current look; and the tally adds the security proof B lacks. From Revolut take the restraint, not the look.
