# Naming round 7: metaphors for features and feelings, plus animals (Oct 27, 2026)

The owner asked for metaphorical names that relate to the product's features and to how users will feel. The Team Lead later added a second direction: animals whose traits match what the product does. The Product owner mapped both directions, made up about 45 names and screened each with web searches (apps, software brands, US marks in classes 9/42, photo, backup and recovery products). The list leaves out every name used or dropped in rounds 1–6, and anything close to **PHOTO home**, our nearest competitor. These are web searches, not legal clearance. The attorney search (US and Philippines) still comes before the beta.

## 1. Map: features and feelings to metaphors

| Feature | Metaphor families |
|---|---|
| Finds real photos in a nameless pile | Gold in a seam, a tide leaving things on the shore, a dog gathering a scattered flock |
| Sets junk aside, deletes nothing | A dog that brings every sheep in and loses none; a chest where nothing is thrown out |
| Dated timeline (year/month) | The tide line (one line along the shore), honeycomb cells in order |
| Stays on your computer | A porch, a home, a hive, a chest: things kept at home, nothing sent out |
| Explains every decision | A light left on, so you can see |

| Feeling | Metaphor families |
|---|---|
| Relief after loss | Calm water after a storm, a light still on when you get home |
| Reassurance, safety, trust | A sheepdog's gentle watch, a chest that holds family things |
| "My memories are back" | Things left on the shore, the gold found in rubble |
| Calm order after chaos | Honeycomb, a tide going out |

Rule for every name: no promise to recover or repair anything, no cloud or upload meaning, and no hint of cleaning, wiping or deleting (**Never destructive**, voice.md).

## 2. Dropped (39)

**Metaphor names (25)**
- **Taken in our field:** Mantel (the "Mantel: Family Album" app), Quilt (several photo-quilt apps), Locket (photo-widget app), Bookend (security software; the Bookends app), Afterglow (photo editor, now AfterLight), First Light (Filmic's Firstlight camera app), Hearth (Hearth Display family photo calendar), Flowerpress (Flower Press Journal, a memory-journal app), Dovetail (research software company), Weft (an existing app).
- **Crowded searches:** Harbor (software toolkits, and a US/UK spelling split), Keel/Even Keel (a Keel app; the idiom is unfamiliar), Beachcomber (beachcombing apps).
- **Wrong meaning:** Clearing (sits among the "photo cleaner" apps), Darning (means repair; a craft app), Sunpatch ("patch" means fix), Driftline (a rip-current app; "drift" means lost), Landfall (hurricanes), Seedbank (seed-inventory apps).
- **Weak link or unfamiliar:** Mooring (marina software), Strandline (obscure), Windowsill (weak link to the product), Stillwater (a city; crowded search), Top Drawer (means "posh"; unfamiliar in the Philippines), Lamplight (nothing found, but it adds nothing Porchlight lacks).

**Animal names (14)**
- **Taken:** Magpie (Magpie Photos & Notes), Squirrel (SquirrelSave backup), Otter (Otter.ai marks), Packrat (Dropbox's version-history add-on), Elephant (Evernote's logo), Jay (Jaybird headphones).
- **Conflicts or wrong meaning:** Hedgehog (many HEDGEHOG marks, including Sega's engine), Turtle (Turtl, an encrypted notes app), Chipmunk (voice-changer apps; cute), Swallow (also means "eat up", as if files vanish), Salmon (dies after returning home), Beaver (slang), Dormouse (sleepy).
- **Unfamiliar:** Nuthatch (unknown in the Philippines; "nut").

| Name | Metaphor → feature / feeling | In a sentence | Say / spell risk | Conflicts found | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Porchlight** | The light left on for someone coming home → stays on your computer, explains plainly / reassurance, warmth | "Porchlight found 2,214 photos." "Porchlight can't save to that folder. Your files are fine." | Low. Some will type "Porch Light" | No software product found. Porch.com (home services) uses "Porch" | Low–medium |

## 4. Animal shortlist

| Name | Trait → feature / feeling | Mascot note | Conflicts found | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sheepdog** | Gathers a scattered flock and loses none, without biting → finds every photo, deletes nothing / gentle protection, trust | A calm border collie lying beside a neat row of sheep. No whistle or snarl | SHEEPDOG marks outside software (class 41 training, among others). In the US, "sheepdog" is also slang for an armed protector. No photo software found | Medium |
| **Honeybee** | Sorts what it gathers into neat cells in the hive → year/month folders, kept at home / calm order | No stinger. Hexagon cells can echo the month grid | Only an old (2010) honeybee education app and a HONEY BEE PRINT mark. Close to "Honey" (PayPal's shopping extension) | Low–medium; leans cute |
| **Bowerbird** | Collects found treasures and lays them out in order → finds and arranges photos / pride, "my things are back" | Blue objects in a tidy arc | **BowerBird is a citizen-science site where people upload nature photos** (Museums Victoria / Atlas of Living Australia). That is the same field, and it implies uploading | Medium–high. Spelling risk too: "Bauer", "Power" |

## 5. The team's top 2

1. **Porchlight.** It is the warmest name this round with no wrong meaning: nothing about cleaning, the cloud or repair. It says "home" (fixing round 6's "Homecoming is vague") through a picture, not a promise. It is also the cleanest search, and it reads well in error lines.
2. **Sheepdog.** It is the only animal that carries **Never destructive** in its nature: it gathers everything and harms nothing. It is also a strong mascot. The US "armed protector" meaning needs checking.

Reserve: **Tideline**. It is lovely but carries the detergent risk in our main market. Goldseam is the best low-risk backup if the attorney search is the deciding factor.

## 6. What to test (add to D21)

- **"What do you think this app does?"** A name fails if 2 or more of 5 testers say it recovers, deletes, cleans, uploads or shares photos. For Tideline, also ask "Is this a cleaning product?" For Sheepdog, ask "Is this security or guard software?"
- **Hear it, type it.** Pass: 6 of 8 type it exactly, with at least 3 of 4 Filipino testers (as in T44).
- **Read the real sentences**, including the error line ("___ can't save to that folder") and a friend's recommendation.
- **Keepsake stays the control.** Test no more than one animal name per session, so the mascot appeal doesn't split the signal.

The name is the owner's call (D21).
