# Naming round 21: more "sort" names (Nov 10, 2026)

The owner asked for more names with "sort" as a prefix or suffix. The UX writer made up 34 new names (none used in rounds 1–20), checked sound, spelling, meaning and honesty, and shortlisted 10. The Product owner web-screened those 10 (apps, companies, US marks via third-party mirrors, search crowding). Not legal clearance. Domains, IPOPHL and WIPO couldn't be checked from here.

## Findings first

1. **"Sort" is a crowded word.** It shows up in games (Water Sort, Ball Sort, Good Sort), Sortly (home inventory, live US marks in classes 9 and 42, Reg. 7106436), planners (Sorted, Sortd), sorting algorithms (Timsort, tree sort) and many SORT- marks (SORTED, SORTIT, SORTIFY.TM, SORTSY…). The word gives little protection, so the other half of the name does the work.
2. **"Sort-" can be heard as "Short-"** in Filipino English. Names where "short + word" is a real phrase fail (Sortway, Sortlane). Names ending in "sort" avoid it but mostly hit finding 1.
3. **The join decides typing:** t-m, t-n, t-p, t-w are safe; t-t, t-d, t-th, t-s, f and v joins aren't.
4. **The test already has four sort names** (Sortwell, Sortory, SortnOrg, Sortlee). A new one only adds variety if it replaces one. Sortlee is the natural slot: it sits in Sortly's shadow.

**Dropped before the screen (24):** e.g. Sortrow ("sorrow"), Sortrest ("rest in peace"), Sortbin (rubbish bin), Ownsort (heard "Unsort"), Sorten ("shorten"), Timesort and Treesort (algorithms), Clearsort ("clear out"), Sorttime (double t), Sortframe and Sortgrove (f/p, v/b), Sortright (accuracy claim).

**Candid view (writer):** none of these is clearly stronger than Sortwell or Sortory, which are already in the test. Sortnest is the best new candidate; Sortmate adds the only new idea (a friend) but has a same-name app.

## If Sortnest joins the test (T44)

- Block rule clash: Sortory, SortnOrg and a new sort name must each sit in a different block, which two blocks can't do. So Sortnest either replaces SortnOrg in Block A (simplest), or replaces Sortlee with a short third block for SortnOrg. The owner chooses (D38).
- Hear-it, type-it fails: "Short-" in any form, Sortness, Shortest, Sort Nest.
- Extra question: "Is this an app for organizing your house or the things you own?" Fails if 2 or more of 5 say yes.
