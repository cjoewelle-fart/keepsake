# Naming round 9: life bubbles, and playback (Oct 27, 2026)

The owner asked for two new directions: (A) "life bubbles", where a bubble holds each photo safely while it is sorted, and (B) "playback", which helps people remember their photos. The Product owner listed what each idea brings with it, made up 47 names and checked each one with web searches (apps, software brands, US marks in classes 9/42, photo, backup and recovery products). The list leaves out every name used or dropped in rounds 1–8. These are web searches, not legal clearance. The attorney search still comes before the beta (phase 4).

## Two findings first

1. **"Bubble" is a crowded word in software.**
   - Bubble Group, Inc. (bubble.io) has filed BUBBLE marks for software platforms, both downloadable (class 9) and online (class 42).
   - Search results for every "Bubble-" name are full of bubble.io pages. There are also Bubble apps for childcare and productivity.
   - Most "bubble" apps are games where you pop bubbles. So popping is what people link to bubbles, and popping means loss.
2. **MemoryBubble is a Mac cleaner app** where you double-click a bubble to "pop" it and free memory. That is exactly the "pop = delete" meaning we have to avoid.

## A. What bubbles bring with them

| Attribute | Feature or feeling | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Holds one thing whole inside | Each photo kept whole; nothing changed | Helps |
| "Your own bubble", private | Nothing leaves your computer (**Private by architecture**) | Helps |
| Bubble wrap | Protecting precious things | Helps, but the word is taken (see Dropped) |
| Rises to the surface | Real photos come up out of the pile | Helps |
| Round frame or lens | Each photo in its own frame | Mixed: our tiles are rectangles |
| Light, gentle | Calm | Mixed: can tip into cute |
| Rainbow sheen | Memories, colour | Neutral |
| Bubbles cluster | Months and years grouped together | Helps, but weakly |
| Soap, washing | "Clean": voice.md bans it, since set-aside files are never cleaned away | **Fights** |
| "Fixed" inside | We don't repair photos | **Fights** |
| Pops, vanishes, fragile | Loss, the opposite of keeping (**Never destructive**) | **Fights, badly** |
| Filter, speech, money bubbles | Echo chamber, chat, market crash | **Fights** |

**How to keep the good and lose the bad:** lean on "a protective bubble" and "rising to the surface". Never mention soap, washing or fixing. Never show or animate a bubble popping: set-aside files should settle, not burst. Put "keep" in the name so it can't be read as fragile.

## A. Dropped (25)

- **Taken in our field:**
  - Memorybubble: a 2016 photo-sharing app, plus the MemoryBubble Mac cleaner (finding 2).
  - Photobubble: old PHOTOBUBBLES and MYPHOTOBUBBLE marks (status not checked), a Photo Bubble Level app, and Fotobabble.
  - Bubbleframe: a BubbleFrame photo-editing app.
  - Pixbubble, Picbubble: "Pic Bubbler" was a notorious "see anyone naked" app.
  - Bubblepod: a 360° camera stand with its own app.
  - Bubblelens: Bubblescope phone lens, Bubble Camera app.
- **Taken elsewhere:**
  - Bubblewrap: BUBBLE WRAP is a registered trademark (Sealed Air), and bubblewrap is the Linux sandboxing tool behind Flatpak.
  - Bubbleup: a BubbleUp company and a Bubble Up app.
  - Homebubble: a French app for household paperwork.
  - Mybubble: MyBubble Ltd, a MyBubble mood-tracker app.
  - Bubblenest: a BubbleNest friend-finding app.
  - Bubbly: a voice social network with 30M users.
  - Bubblepost: Bubble Post, a Belgian delivery firm (it sends things away).
  - Timebubble, Bubbletime: TimeBubble, a memory-journal app, and Bubble Time timer apps.
  - Surface, Surfacing: Microsoft Surface.
- **Wrong meaning or weak:**
  - Bubblesort: a textbook sorting algorithm (same problem as Combsort).
  - Safebubble: "safe" is a claim voice.md avoids, and search shows bubble.io security pages.
  - Clearbubble, Bubbleglass: "clear" means clean.
  - Bubblebox: "-box" reads as a delete app (round 5).
  - Snapbubble: echoes Snapchat and Pic Bubbler.
  - Bubblelight: Christmas bubble lamps, and light names are already covered by Keeplight.
  - Bubble Album, Bubbleline: search shows only speech-bubble caption apps and bubble.io guides.
  - Bubblekeep: reads as an order ("bubble, keep!"), the weaker twin of Keepbubble.
  - Upwell: an ocean term, not a bubble word for most users.

## A. Bubble shortlist

| Name | Attribute → feature or feeling | In a sentence | Say / spell risk | Conflicts found | Risk | Mascot / visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Keepbubble** | A protective bubble you keep → each photo whole, on your computer / calm safety | "Keepbubble found 2,214 photos." "Keepbubble can't save to that folder. Your files are fine." | Low–medium: "Keep Bubble", "Keepbubbles" | No product found. bubble.io crowds the search | Medium | A clear bubble around a photo tile in the logo only. Tiles in the app stay rectangular |
| **Lifebubble** (the owner's idea) | Your life in its own bubble → private memories | "Lifebubble found 2,214 photos." Reads like a life-tracking app | Low | Life360 (a big family app) has a "Bubbles" feature that ends after a set time. A TimeBubble memory app. No LIFEBUBBLE mark found | Medium–high | Same, but "life" makes it sound like a lifestyle or insurance app |
| **Bubblefold** | Bubble + folder → each photo kept safe, then filed by date | "Bubblefold found 2,214 photos." | Medium: "Bubble Fold" | None found | Medium | Bubbles landing in folders. "Fold" can suggest laundry (round 2) |
| **Keepwrap** | Bubble wrap without the word "bubble" → protecting precious things | "Keepwrap found 2,214 photos." "Keepwrap can't save…" | Low | No software found. Food-wrap products | Low–medium | Photo tiles wrapped in soft padding. It may read as a food-storage brand |
| **Bubbleshelf** | Bubbles resting on a shelf → photos set out by date | "Bubbleshelf found 2,214 photos." | Low | None found in software | Medium | A shelf of round frames. Cute risk |

**The team's top 2 (bubble):**

1. **Keepbubble.** It keeps the owner's picture (a protective bubble around each photo) and adds "keep", which cancels the popping and fragility. It carries on from Keepsake and Keeplight. Its risk is the bubble.io search clutter, not any product we found.
2. **Keepwrap.** It is the "bubble wrap" idea without bubble.io and without popping. It has the cleanest search, but the bubble is only implied.

Lifebubble is weaker than both. It isn't clear what the app does from the name, and it sits next to Life360's Bubbles feature in the same family-app space.

## B. Playback: map and risks

| Idea | Feature or feeling |
|---|---|
| Rewind to a date | The year/month/day timeline |
| The timeline as a reel | Scrolling through your past in order |
| Press play on your past | Seeing what came back (**good news first**) |
| Encore, replay | "My memories are back" |

**Risks:**
- "Play" promises a player or slideshow, but the app sorts and saves. Most video tiles even say "Can't play in this browser", so a playback name contradicts the app's own screens.
- Media-player apps crowd every "play" search.
- Google Photos and Apple Photos "Memories" own the "relive your past" feeling, and both are cloud features.

- Rewind: the AI memory app, now Limitless, bought by Meta.
- Replay: Replay.io, a developer tool.
- Flashback: Photo Flashback, Flashbacks and Flashback memory apps.
- Encore: Encore: Concert Diary, plus Encore Memories, Inc. marks.
- Photoreel: Adobe's "Photo Reels" feature.
- Dayreel: a DayReel daily-photo app.
- Reelback: Reelback.com marks and a video-clipping app.
- Memory Lane: a Memory Lane photo app and Ente's cloud feature.
- Remember When: Friends Reunited's memory app.
- Lookback: Lookback.io, a user-research tool.
- Retrace: Stackify's code-monitoring tool.
- Rerun: a Strava add-on.
- Recall: Windows Recall, an AI feature with a privacy controversy.
- Filmstrip: a feature in the Windows Photos app.

## B. Playback shortlist

| Name | Idea → feature or feeling | In a sentence | Say / spell risk | Conflicts found | Risk | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Playback** (the owner's idea) | Press play on your past → the timeline / remembering | "Playback found 2,214 photos." "Playback can't save…" sounds like a media error | Low | Many Playback apps (MultiTracks for church music, Playback Now), and a generic word | High | A play triangle, which promises a slideshow |
| **Keepreel** | A reel you keep → the dated timeline | "Keepreel found 2,214 photos." | Medium: "Keep Real", "Keepreal" | Only ReelKeep, a fishing-reel app. "Reel" now means Instagram Reels, which suggests a video maker | Medium | A film strip of tiles |
| **Homereel** | Home movies, kept at home | "Homereel found 2,214 photos." | Medium: "Home Real" | Real-estate reel-maker tools | Medium | Weak: sounds like a video app |

Playback's strength is a feeling (remembering), not what the app does. It fits better as a premium feature after launch, such as a slideshow or "On this day", than as the name.

## Overall view across rounds

- **Bubble vs earlier picks.** The bubble direction gives a lovely visual (photos held safely inside round frames), but the word fights two promises (popping means loss, soap means cleaning) and bubble.io crowds every search. Keepbubble is the best bubble name, but it doesn't beat **Photocomb** (it says what the app does) or **Keeplight** (warm, same "keep" root, cleaner search) as a name.
- **Recommended use.** Make Keepbubble the reserve name in place of Lamplighter, and use the "protective bubble" as a **visual idea** under any winning name, for the logo and empty screens.
- **Top picks across both directions this round:** 1. Keepbubble, 2. Keepwrap. Playback isn't recommended as a name.
- **Test (add to D21), using T44's rules:**
  - Ask "What do you think this app does?" A name fails if 2 or more of 5 testers say it cleans, deletes, repairs or plays photos.
  - For bubble names, also ask "Would the photos pop or disappear?"
  - Hear it, type it: 6 of 8 type it exactly, with at least 3 of 4 Filipino testers.
  - Keepsake stays the control.

The name is the owner's call (D21), and it must be settled before the beta (phase 4).
