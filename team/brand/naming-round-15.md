# Naming round 15: your edits (Oct 28, 2026)

The owner asked for four changes on Oct 28: drop Photocomb and find plain words to replace "comb"; replace Ringwood with plainer tree-ring names; check "Sorty" in place of Sortwell; and find more names like Reshelf. The Product owner made up 48 names and checked each one with web searches (apps, software brands, US marks in classes 9/42, photo, backup, recovery and sorting products). The list leaves out every name used or dropped in rounds 1–14. These are web searches, not legal clearance. The attorney search still comes before the beta (phase 4).

## Findings first

1. **Sorty is taken in our own field.** Sorty is a free, open-source (GPL-3.0) Mac app that sorts a folder's files into named folders, with "offline operation, no tracking". That is almost our own pitch. There is also an older Sorty photo tagger for Mac that moves photos into folders, a Sorty Match 3D sorting game, Sorty for Spotify and a sorty-app.com inventory tool. IRI, a data-sorting software company, has a mark called **SortI**, which sounds the same. Verdict: **don't use it.**
2. **Reshelf sounds like RedShelf.** RedShelf is an e-textbook platform, now part of VitalSource. Heard aloud, Reshelf and RedShelf are one sound apart, and searches for "Rightshelf" get changed to RedShelf. That isn't our field, but the hear-it-type-it test must check for it.
3. **Most plain "search" words are taken by photo apps:** pick, find, spot, scout, hunt, browse, scan and sieve. "Pick" also means choosing favourites (PicPicks, Google's Photo Picker), which hints that the rest gets dropped (**Never destructive**).

## 3. Sorty, and "sort + short"

**Verdict on Sorty: high risk, drop it** (finding 1). It has two more problems:
- **Meaning:** "Sorty" reads as "sorta" (only half-sorted), and "shorty" is slang. Green's Dictionary of Slang lists "sorty" as an old colloquial word.
- **Sound:** the plain "t" in Filipino English could make "Sorty can't save…" sound like "Sorry can't save…". This needs the test to confirm.

| Name | Idea | In a sentence | Say / spell risk | Conflicts found | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sortwell** (keep) | Sorted well | "Sortwell found 2,214 photos." "Sortwell can't save to that folder. Your files are fine." | Low–medium | As in round 13 | Low–medium |
| **Shortsort** | Sort + short, said literally | "Shortsort found 2,214 photos." | **High: the sh/s tongue-twister.** Also "shorts" (clothing), and "falls short" | No product or mark found | Medium |

**Recommendation:** keep Sortwell. Put "short" in the copy instead of the name, as a fact: "40,000 files sorted in under 5 minutes". Publish that line only after the phase 4 gate measures it on real hardware (voice.md: "State facts, not feelings").

## The team's proposed test set (D21, T44 rules)

| Slot | Name | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Control | **Keepsake** | No change |
| Owner's favourite | **Unjumble** | No change |
| Literal, result | **Reshelf** | Takes Photocomb's slot (round 14 named it the replacement) |
| Search word | **Sortwell** | Stays. Sorty failed screening |
| Years in order | **Yeartree** | Takes Ringwood's slot |

- **Sessions:** test Unjumble and Reshelf in different sessions (round 14). Test at most one -well name and one tree name per session.
- **"What do you think this app does?"** A name fails if 2 or more of 5 testers say it recovers, deletes, cleans or uploads photos. For Yeartree, also ask "Is this a family-tree app?" For Reshelf, also ask "Would it move or hide your photos?"
- **Hear it, type it:** pass is 6 of 8 typed exactly, with at least 3 of 4 Filipino testers. Count "Year Three", "Redshelf" and "Sortwel" as fails.
