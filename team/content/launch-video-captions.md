# Launch video captions (Nov 11, second cut)

Burned-in captions for the launch films in `video/`. The text lives in `video/launch-film/assets/captions.js`, where `{APP}` comes from `src/brand.ts`. The UX writer rewrote them after the owner's notes on Nov 11:
- say what the product does, not what we found;
- never imply the whole computer is looked at, because it reads only the folder you choose;
- no numbers from the test folder;
- no Taglish version (dropped).

## 16:9 film (~40 s)

| Beat | Caption |
|---|---|
| 1a Hook | After a recovery, files come back like this. |
| 1b Hook | Strange names, small previews, copies, damaged files. |
| 2 Choose folder | Choose your recovery folder, and {APP} sorts it. |
| 3 Scan | It reads only that folder, on your computer. |
| 4 Set aside | Set aside, not deleted. You can keep any of them. |
| 5 By date | Sorted by date taken. Unselect what you don't want. |
| 6 Save | You save new copies, sorted by year and month. |
| 7 End card (approved) | Your photos stay on your computer. / Nothing is deleted. / Made for recovered files. / Free, and it runs in your browser. |

## 9:16 cut (15 s)

| Beat | Caption |
|---|---|
| Hook | Recovered files look like this. |
| Sort | {APP} sorts them in your browser. |
| Set aside | Set aside, not deleted. |
| By date | Sorted by the date taken. |

The writer's alternative for beat 3, if you want "stop any time" in it: "It reads only that folder. You can stop anytime."
