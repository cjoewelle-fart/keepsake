### How it works

**1. Choose your recovery folder**
Pick the folder your recovery program made. It's the one that holds recup_dir.1, recup_dir.2 and so on. Upsort reads it on your computer and never changes it.

**2. Sorted by date**
Upsort finds your photos and videos and puts them in order by the date each one was taken. Small preview copies, exact copies, damaged files and app images are set aside, never deleted. You can keep any of them.

**3. Check and save**
Look through, unselect anything you don't want, and save. Upsort makes new copies in a folder you choose, with a folder for each year and month. Photos with no date go in a "No date" folder. Your recovery folder stays as it was.

### Questions

**Does it recover my photos?**
No. Upsort doesn't search your memory card or phone for lost photos. A recovery program like PhotoRec does that part. Upsort's job is to sort the files your recovery program has already found. Haven't used one yet? Our free guide shows you how, step by step.

**Will it change or delete my files?**
No. Upsort only reads your recovery folder. Files it sets aside stay exactly where they are, and you can keep any of them. When you save, it makes new copies in a folder you choose. You'll need some free space for them.

**Are my photos uploaded?**
No. Upsort runs inside your browser, on your computer. Your photos are never sent to us or anyone else. There's no account to make.

**What do I need?**
A computer and the folder your recovery program made. Upsort is free. It works best in Chrome or Edge. In other browsers, your photos are saved as zip files instead of straight into a folder.

**Which recovery programs does it work with?**
Upsort is made and tested for folders from PhotoRec, a free recovery program. You can choose a folder from another recovery program too, but we haven't tested those yet.

**Why is it called Upsort?**
It's like upcycling. Upsort turns the jumble of files a recovery program gives back into a photo library you can use. It all happens on your computer.

## 4. Don't say

Claims we must not make in any public copy (launch page, listings, posts, replies).

**About recovery and results**
- "Recovers", "restores", "rescues" or "brings back" your photos. Upsort only sorts files. It doesn't recover them.
- "All your photos", "every photo", "get your photos back", "your memories are safe now".
- "Fixes" or "repairs" damaged photos or videos. We set them aside. We don't repair them.
- "Guaranteed", "100%", "always", "perfectly accurate", "never makes mistakes".
- "Works with any recovery program". Only PhotoRec output is tested.
- Numbers from the pilot or tests (for example "found 2,214 photos") unless the source and date are next to them.
- "Fast", "instant", "in seconds". Speed depends on the computer and the folder.
- "Nothing to buy" or "free forever". A paid version is planned later.

**About how it works**
- "AI", "smart", "intelligent", "learns", "magic", "powerful", "effortless", "seamless".
- Sorting by faces, events, places or people. It sorts by date only.
- "Duplicates", "thumbnails", "junk", "metadata", "EXIF", "hash". Say copies, small preview copies, app images, date taken.
- "Removes", "deletes", "cleans up" or "filters out" files. Say "sets aside".
- "Works on your phone" or "mobile app". It's for computers.
- "Download the app", or "a Windows or Mac app". It runs in the browser. (Installing it is optional and only in Chrome and Edge.)
- "Full support in Safari and Firefox" without saying that saving happens as zip files there.

**About privacy and safety**
- Anything that suggests a server or the cloud: "upload your folder", "we process your photos", "sync", "back up", "sign in", "your account", "stored securely".
- "Secure", "military-grade", "100% private", "bank-level", "peace of mind", "worry-free". State the fact instead: "never uploaded", "only reads your folder".
- "Never touches your files". Saving does write new copies, so say "never changes or deletes your files".
- "Works offline" or "turn off your Wi-Fi". Hold until QA and the owner sign off for public copy (voice rule 4).
- "Open source" or "read the code". Hold until the LICENSE file is in and the repo is public.

**About other products**
- Never name a competitor or compare ourselves with one, by name or by hint ("unlike other apps", "the others delete your files").
- "The only", "the first", "the best". These can't be checked, and they're comparisons too.
