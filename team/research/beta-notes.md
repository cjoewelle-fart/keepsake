## Rules checked that passed

- **color-contrast / contrast-feedback (§1, §8):** walked every rendered text node on all 14 screens at all 3 viewports, blending opacity and the backgrounds behind it. 0 pairs were below 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text). That includes the disabled Save, the "Stopping…" state, the `.chip-n` counts at 0.8 opacity on chosen pills, the problem card and the vermilion CTA (white on #C2410C is 5.2:1). There are no placeholders or text inputs.
- **touch-target-size (§2) / touch-friendly-input (§8):** 0 targets under 44x44 on the phone and tablet across every screen (the select is 44 px tall). On desktop, the only ones under 44 are the 36 px pills (UX8). None are under 24 px anywhere.
- **Viewport / zoom:** `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0`, with no `user-scalable=no` or `maximum-scale`. Pinch-zoom works. There's no 300 ms tap delay with this meta.
- **focus-states / keyboard-nav / skip-links / heading-hierarchy / aria-labels:** a11y-check passes 442 checks at 6ac1efc.
- **focus-not-obscured (§1):** 150 Tab stops in review at 390x844, 768x1024 and 1280x800. 0 were fully under a sticky bar; the worst partial overlap was 1%. Exception: UX1, at 720-785 px high.
- **reduced-motion (§1):** with `reduce`, the start, results and review screens (after a year chip) had 0 running animations. The count-up shows 323 at once, and `scroll-behavior` is auto. Without it, the start screen had 4 animations, and none loops.
- **escape-routes (§1):** Stop scanning, Stop saving, viewer Close and Esc, Back to review and Back to Sort all work. Focus starts on Close when the viewer opens and returns to the photo last shown when it closes (checked after Esc, after Close, and after → then Esc, desktop and phone).
- **gesture-alternative / gesture-conflicts / swipe-clarity / standard-gestures (§2):** there are no swipe-only actions (the viewer uses Previous/Next buttons and ← →), no horizontal scrolling on the phone, and no drag interactions.
- **error-placement / error-clarity / error-recovery / aria-live-errors / focus-management (§8):** "folder refused" shows a `role="alert"` card directly above the folder card. Focus goes to its heading. The text gives the cause and the fix ("…didn't allow it, or the folder is protected… Try a folder in Pictures or Documents"), and the button becomes "Choose a different folder".
- **disabled-states (§8):** the empty Save uses `aria-disabled`, `cursor: not-allowed`, a changed fill (ink-soft on --rule, 5.7:1) and `aria-describedby` with the reason.
- **empty-states (§8):** an empty folder, nothing worth keeping, and a "Not selected" filter with nothing in it each show a message and an action.
- **multi-step-progress (§8):** a stepper on every step ("Step 3 of 4 · Choose" on the phone), with back navigation.
- **input-labels / form-labels (§8):** the month select has a visible wrapping label ("Jump to a month"), and every checkbox has a name. There are no text inputs, so required-indicators, inline-validation, autofill and password don't apply.
- **loading-buttons / submit-feedback (§2, §8):** scanning and saving have progress bars, percentage live text and Stop buttons. The one gap is UX9.
- **toast-dismiss / toast-accessibility:** there are no toasts. Changes are announced in polite status regions, and focus doesn't move.
